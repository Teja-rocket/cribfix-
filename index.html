The Problem

Maintenance chaos ends here

Most apartment buildings handle maintenance informally — a WhatsApp message here, a phone call there. Complaints get buried, forgotten, or never followed up.

Residents raise an issue and then have no idea whether it was seen, assigned, or resolved. There's no record, no accountability, and no peace of mind. CribFix fixes that by giving every complaint a home.

How It Works

Simple four-step flow

A simple four-step flow that keeps everyone in the loop.

1

Resident Raises a Complaint

Fill in the form with a title, category, priority, and description. Saved immediately.

2

Admin Reviews & Acknowledges

The building admin sees the new ticket and moves it to "In Progress" when work begins.

3

Notes Are Added Along the Way

Admins attach updates so the resident always knows what is happening.

4

Issue Marked Resolved

Once done, the complaint is marked Resolved. The resident can see the final status at any time.
