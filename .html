CribFix

A simple way to manage apartment maintenance complaints — no more lost WhatsApp messages.

The Problem

Most apartment buildings manage maintenance issues through informal channels — a WhatsApp group here, a phone call there. Complaints get buried in chat history, forgotten by staff, or simply never followed up.

Residents raise an issue and then have no idea whether it was seen, assigned, or resolved. There's no record, no accountability, and no peace of mind. CribFix fixes that by giving every complaint a home.

Who Uses CribFix?

The system is designed around two clear roles, each with a focused set of actions.

🏠
Resident

Raise a new maintenance complaint, describe the issue, pick a category, and then track its status in real time — right from a simple dashboard.

🛠️
Admin / Staff

View all complaints across the building, update their status as work progresses, and add notes so residents always know what is happening.

Key Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't. CribFix keeps things focused and easy to use.

  • Submit a complaint with a title, description, and category
  • Three clear statuses: Open, In Progress, and Resolved
  • Admins can add internal notes to each complaint
  • Residents can view all their own complaints in one place
  • Admin dashboard shows all complaints across the building
  • Status updates are reflected immediately — no refreshing needed

How It Works

A simple four-step flow that keeps everyone in the loop.

1

Resident Raises a Complaint

Fill in the complaint form with a title, description, and category. The complaint is saved and immediately visible to admins.

2

Admin Reviews & Acknowledges

The building admin or maintenance staff sees the new ticket on their dashboard and moves it to "In Progress" when work begins.

3

Notes Are Added Along the Way

Admins can attach notes — such as "Plumber arriving Thursday" — so the resident always knows what is happening with their issue.

4

Issue Marked Resolved

Once work is complete, the complaint is marked Resolved. The resident can see the final status and any closing notes at any time.
